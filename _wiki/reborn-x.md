## What is Reborn?

Before getting into Reborn X, it is important to understand what Reborn actually means.

Project Redstoneworld was originally started by TheJoCraft in 2013 as a build project. In 2016, the project got a new leader, Ij, marking an important point in Redstoneworld's history.

During this period, Redstoneworld began developing into the larger project it is today. The world was moved to America and English, Tower 1 was completed, and many of the foundations for the future of Redstoneworld were established.

This new stage became known as **Reborn**. The name represented the continuation and rebuilding of Redstoneworld under its new direction. 

**Reborn X** continues this era after nearly ten years of Reborn. Through the Reborn X Museum, we aim to preserve and present the history of Redstoneworld and the many developments that have shaped it along the way.

## Reborn X

Reborn X was a major Redstoneworld event celebrating ten years of the Reborn era. The event included the opening of the Reborn X Museum, new minigames, an Announcement Special, and extensive preparation and follow-up work surrounding the event.

### Event

The Reborn X event brought together several projects that had been prepared specifically for the occasion. The main part of the event was the opening of the **Reborn X Museum**, created to preserve and present the history of Redstoneworld.

The event introduced the opening of Minigame Center, as well as the **Reborn X Announcement Special**. **The Curator** also became available during the event, giving players a new way to interact with the museum and its content.

A large amount of preparation went into the event before its opening, with additional development and work continuing afterwards to finish projects and complete the remaining details. Reborn X therefore covered a wider period of preparation, activities, and follow-up work rather than being limited to the event itself.

It showcases all seven RW Chapters, major projects and sectors, along with a map, images, posters, and other historical material.

The museum also includes a dedicated Project Exhibition covering major projects such as Tower 1, {% include wiki-link.html title="Redstone Tower Complex (RTC) Sector" %}, {% include wiki-link.html title="RCorp Rail" %}, 	{% include wiki-link.html title="Theme Park" %}, and the museum itself. The basement contains additional exhibits, the museum credits, and  [The Curator](#the-curator), an interactive character focused on Redstoneworld lore.

The museum was proposed in March 2026, built over the following months, and officially opened on July 10th, 2026 as part of the Reborn X event. 

### The Curator

The Curator was introduced as part of Reborn X through the **Curator Lore**. During the event, players could interact with The Curator and receive different tasks to complete.

These tasks gradually revealed more of The Curator's story and gave players an additional storyline to follow during Reborn X. The Curator Lore became one of the event's interactive parts.
