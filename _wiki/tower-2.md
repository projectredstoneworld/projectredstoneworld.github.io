*Tower 2 is part of the {% include wiki-link.html title="Redstone Tower Complex (RTC) Sector" %}*

Tower 2 is the older of the two RTC towers. In an old video from July 11th, 2017 (see <a href="/wiki/ijs-devlogs/#rebornre2">Reborn Revisited Ep. 2</a>), it is mentioned that the original release date for the Redstoneworld world download was July 10th of that year. This release date was missed due to the addition of this tower, which at the time was an addition to {% include wiki-link.html title="Founding Island" %}.
