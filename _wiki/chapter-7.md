# Chapter 7 - Growth and Completion
**May 2024 – Present**

## The Growth of Redstoneworld

### A true 24/7 server

Chapter 7 began after Hudson rebuilt the hardware of the **24/7 Redstoneworld project** in April 2024. This allowed Redstoneworld to operate as a true 24/7 server rather than only being available when a staff member was online.

The change brought a noticeable increase in activity. A new group of players, often referred to as the **Plotworld Gooners**, became active on the server, with **Powercascade, Noah, and LLucas** being among the most notable additions during mid to late 2024.

### A growing community

With the server becoming more consistently active, Redstoneworld's playerbase began to grow alongside its development. New players brought additional activity to the world while the existing team continued working on the long-term plans that had been established during previous chapters.

The increased activity also helped Redstoneworld feel less like a project that was simply being maintained and more like an active world that was continuously being expanded.

## Redstone Tower Complex

### Tower 3.0

Tower 3.0 received major development during Chapter 7 and eventually became part of its own sector, the **Redstone Tower Complex**.

Development of the area has remained a major focus throughout the chapter, with new sections continuing to be added and existing plans being expanded. Work that had been planned for the towers for years could finally be carried out on a much larger scale.

The towers had previously been one of Redstoneworld's more uncertain projects, but Chapter 7 turned them into a much more active and developed part of the world.

## Completing the World

### Lore and infrastructure

{% include wiki-image.html file="wiki/assets/images/2026-08-27_21.13.59.webp" caption="Anticatalyst and Catalyst Probe" side="left" %} Redstoneworld also developed a much more sophisticated **lore system** during this chapter. The world was no longer only a collection of builds and sectors, but increasingly had its own history and internal context connecting different areas together.

The **RCorp Rail** network continued to expand across the world, connecting the different sectors and giving the world a more coherent transportation system.

### Theme Park expansion

The Theme Park continued to receive development as well. Chambo had Sean complete his **Waterpark expansion** to the Theme Park before eventually taking over the project as its Project Manager.

This allowed another long-running area of Redstoneworld to continue expanding while the rest of the world was being developed around it.

## Long-Term Development

### Keeping the plans moving

One of the defining characteristics of Chapter 7 has been the continued focus on finishing older plans instead of constantly moving on to completely new ones.

Projects that had existed as ideas for years were now receiving actual development, while existing sectors continued to be expanded. The combination of an active playerbase, regular development, and better-connected infrastructure allowed Redstoneworld to keep progressing without losing focus.

## Reborn X

### A new era of Redstoneworld

{% include wiki-image.html file="wiki/assets/images/2026-06-09_21.05.45.webp" caption="The Reborn X Museum" side="right" %} As Chapter 7 continued, the project increasingly moved toward the idea of **Reborn X**. The name represents the current era of Redstoneworld and the continuation of the long-term plans that have developed throughout its history.

With the server becoming more active, major projects approaching completion, the railway network expanding, and the world gaining a deeper lore, Redstoneworld entered a period of strong optimism about its future.

The goals established for Chapter 7 appear increasingly achievable, with many of the project's long-running plans finally approaching completion. **Reborn X** represents the continuation of that development and the next stage of Redstoneworld's ongoing history. The history of RW is showcased ingame in the {% include wiki-link.html title="Reborn X Museum" %} .
