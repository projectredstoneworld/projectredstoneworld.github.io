Chapter 3
**Mid to late 2019**

## The Return to Redstoneworld

### A new period of development

{% include wiki-image.html file="wiki/assets/images/Redstone_Park_-_Copy.webp" caption="Official Theme Park Render)" side="left" %}

Chapter 3 was a shorter chapter following a period of discontinuity. It marked a return to active development on Redstoneworld-related projects, with the **Theme Park** becoming the primary focus.

Development continued across the Theme Park, with new areas and improvements being made while additional plans were created for the future of the project.

### Expanding the vision

Although the chapter was relatively short, several ideas developed during this period would later become important parts of Redstoneworld.

The focus was not limited to immediate construction. Plans were made for larger developments that would eventually be implemented during later chapters, laying further groundwork for the project's future expansion.

## The Formation of the Staff Team

### A new generation of staff

Chapter 3 also saw the formation of the **staff and build team**. This marked an important change in how Redstoneworld's development was organized, moving toward a more structured team-based approach.

Among the most notable new members were **Sean, Grate, and Walter**, who would go on to contribute to Redstoneworld during its later development.

### The Great Merge

During this chapter, early brainstorming began for what would eventually become **The Great Merge**.

The idea was to bring the various Redstoneworld-related sectors and projects together into a unified world. While the actual merge would not take place until Chapter 4, the initial planning during Chapter 3 established the foundation for what would become one of the project's major milestones.

## Legacy

### Preparing for the Great Merge

Chapter 3 was brief, but it established several important foundations for the chapters that followed.

## Team Progression

*It is notable that, as mentioned earlier, this was the first chapter to actually have an organised build team*

The Theme Park received renewed development, a dedicated staff and build team was formed, new members joined the project, and the first major ideas for **The Great Merge** began to take shape.

The chapter therefore served as a transition between the early Reborn period and the major structural changes that would define Redstoneworld in 2020.
